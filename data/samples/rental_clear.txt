RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made on 1 March 2026 at Dharwad between Mr. Suresh Patil
("the Landlord") and Ms. Ananya Rao ("the Tenant") for the flat at No. 14, Second
Floor, Green Park Apartments, Vidyagiri, Dharwad 580004 ("the Premises").

1. TERM
1.1 This agreement is for a period of eleven (11) months starting on 1 March 2026
and ending on 31 January 2027.
1.2 The agreement may be renewed for a further term only by written consent of
both parties.

2. RENT AND PAYMENT
2.1 The monthly rent shall be Rs. 15,000 (Rupees Fifteen Thousand only), payable
on or before the 5th of each month.
2.2 Rent shall be paid by bank transfer to the account named by the Landlord in
writing.
2.3 If rent is not paid within 5 days of the due date, the Tenant shall pay a late
fee of Rs. 500 per day until payment is received.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
3.1 The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of Rs. 45,000 (Rupees Forty-Five
Thousand only) at the time of signing this agreement.
3.2 The deposit shall be refunded after the Tenant vacates, after deducting any
amounts the Landlord considers reasonable.

4. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
4.1 The Landlord shall be responsible for major structural repairs.
4.2 The Tenant shall handle minor day-to-day maintenance.
4.3 The Tenant shall not make any alteration to the Premises without the prior
written consent of the Landlord.

5. USE OF PREMISES
5.1 The Premises shall be used only for residential purposes.
5.2 The Tenant shall not sublet the Premises or any part of it.

6. TERMINATION
6.1 Either party may terminate this agreement by giving 60 days written notice to
the other party.
6.2 If the Tenant leaves within the first 6 months, the Tenant shall forfeit one
month's rent from the security deposit.

7. GOVERNING TERMS
7.1 Any dispute shall first be discussed between the parties in good faith.
7.2 This agreement is signed in two copies, one for each party.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have signed this agreement on the date above.

Landlord: Suresh Patil                Tenant: Ananya Rao
