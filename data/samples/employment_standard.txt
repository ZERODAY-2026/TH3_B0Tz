EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement is made on 2 June 2026 between Kaveri Software Private
Limited ("the Company") and Mr. Rahul Deshpande ("the Employee").

Clause 1: Position and Start Date
The Employee is appointed as Junior Software Engineer and shall join on 1 July 2026.

Clause 2: Probation
The Employee shall be on probation for six (6) months. During probation either
party may end employment with 15 days written notice.

Clause 3: Salary
The Employee shall receive a gross monthly salary of Rs. 50,000, paid on the last
working day of each month.

Clause 4: Notice Period
After confirmation, either party may terminate employment by giving 90 days written
notice, or salary in lieu of notice at the Company's sole discretion.

Clause 5: Service Bond
If the Employee resigns within 24 months of joining, the Employee shall pay the
Company Rs. 2,00,000 towards training costs.

Clause 6: Non-Compete
For 12 months after leaving, the Employee shall not work for any competitor of the
Company anywhere in India.

Clause 7: Confidentiality
The Employee shall keep all confidential information of the Company secret during
and after employment.

Clause 8: Working Hours
Working hours shall be as decided by the Company from time to time.
