EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Employer: RapidBuild Technologies Pvt. Ltd.
Employee: Mr. Vikram Joshi
Date: 1 August 2026

Clause 1: Position
The Employee is appointed as Marketing Manager, reporting to the Director.

Clause 2: Salary
Gross monthly salary: Rs. 75,000, paid on the last working day of each month.

Clause 3: Termination
Section 3a: The Company may terminate the Employee at any time, for any
reason, with or without cause, and with no notice required.

Section 3b: In the event of termination without cause, the Employee shall
receive 3 months salary as severance.

Section 3c: The Employee may not be terminated without 90 days written
notice and a documented performance improvement plan.

[NOTE: Sections 3a and 3c directly contradict each other. 3a says at-will
with no notice, 3c requires 90 days notice and a PIP.]

Clause 4: Non-Compete
The Employee agrees not to engage in any marketing, advertising, or
consulting work, in any industry, anywhere in India, for 24 months after
leaving the Company.

[NOTE: Unreasonably broad non-compete: all industries, national scope,
24 months.]

Clause 5: Intellectual Property
All ideas, concepts, strategies, and creative works the Employee produces,
including those made on personal time and unrelated to company business,
belong to the Employer.

[NOTE: Overreach: claims ownership of personal, off-duty work product.]

Clause 6: Service Bond
If the Employee resigns within 36 months of joining, the Employee shall
pay the Company Rs. 5,00,000 towards training and opportunity costs.

Clause 7: Working Hours
Working hours shall be as decided by the Company from time to time. The
Company may require additional hours without extra compensation.
