SUBLEASE AGREEMENT

Original Tenant (Sublessor): Ms. Kavitha Nair
New Tenant (Sublessee): Mr. Arun Kumar
Original Landlord: Sai Properties, Bangalore

Clause 1: Premises
The Sublessor subleases Flat 3B, Lotus Enclave, Koramangala, Bangalore
560034, with written consent of the Original Landlord as per Section 12
of the Master Lease.

Clause 2: Term
The sublease runs from 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027 (6 months).
The Master Lease expires on 30 September 2027.

Clause 3: Rent
Monthly rent: Rs. 22,000. The Sublessor's rent under the Master Lease is
Rs. 18,000. The Sublessee pays the Sublessor, who pays the Landlord.

Clause 4: Security Deposit
Rs. 66,000, held by the Sublessor. The Sublessor's deposit with the
Landlord remains unchanged.

Clause 5: Responsibilities
The Sublessee assumes all tenant obligations under the Master Lease for
the sublease term. However, the Sublessor remains liable to the Landlord
if the Sublessee defaults.

Clause 6: Early Termination
If the Sublessee vacates early, the Sublessor may retain the full security
deposit AND require payment of remaining rent through 31 March 2027.

Clause 7: Landlord Approval
This sublease is contingent on written approval from Sai Properties. If
approval is revoked, the sublease terminates with 14 days notice.
